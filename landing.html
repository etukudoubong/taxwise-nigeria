The Problem

Most Nigerians are non-compliant — not because they want to be

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 is 400+ pages. Tax obligations differ by income type, employment status, and business structure. Without a tax accountant — which most people can't afford — staying compliant is genuinely hard.

😰
Freelancers don't know their WHT situation
Clients deduct 10% WHT. Is it a credit or a loss? Most freelancers don't know — and overpay or miss the credit entirely.
🤷
Salaried professionals miss side-income obligations
Your employer handles PAYE on your salary. But that consulting fee or rental income? You owe tax on it — and most people don't declare it.
🏦
Business owners can't separate personal from business
Running everything through one bank account means your expenses, drawings, and business income are all mixed. Tax computation becomes guesswork.
📅
Deadlines are missed and penalties accumulate
WHT by the 21st. PAYE by the 10th. Annual return by 31 March. FIRS penalties compound at 10% per annum. Most people don't track any of this.

TaxWise is a preparation and tracking tool, not a filing platform. GTBank PDFs are protected by your date of birth (DDMMYYYY format). Access Bank uses the last 4 digits of your registered phone number. UBA uses a combination. TaxWise stores your data in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security — only you can see your transactions. TaxWise cannot access your bank account, move money, or change any banking settings.
