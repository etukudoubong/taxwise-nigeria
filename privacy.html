We do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers listed in Section 6. You have full rights over your data under the NDPA 2023.

1. Who We Are

TaxWise Nigeria is a product of Lexi Gravitas Forensic Services, a company registered and operating in Nigeria. We provide AI-powered tax management software for Nigerian individuals and businesses under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (NTA 2025) and Tax Administration Act 2025 (TAA 2025).

Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) is responsible for overseeing compliance with this policy and with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the NDPA 2023, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — You explicitly consent at account creation. You may withdraw consent at any time via the Privacy Control Centre in your Profile settings.
  • Contractual necessity — Processing required to provide the TaxWise service you have subscribed to.
  • Legal obligation — Where we are required by Nigerian law to retain records (e.g., TAA 2025 record-keeping requirements).
  • Legitimate interests — Product analytics and security monitoring, balanced against your privacy rights.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Computing Personal Income Tax (PIT), Companies Income Tax (CIT), VAT, WHT, PAYE and CGT obligations
  • Generating tax schedules, annual summary reports, and filing exports
  • Providing AI-powered tax advice through the TaxWise AI Agent
  • Managing your subscription and processing payments via Paystack
  • Sending transactional emails (account confirmation, trial reminders, payment receipts)
  • Sending product update and tax tip emails only if you have consented to marketing communications
  • Improving our product through aggregated, anonymised usage analytics

5. Camera and File Access

Camera: TaxWise requests camera access solely to allow you to photograph bank statements and financial documents for AI extraction into your transaction journal. No photographs are stored on our servers — only the structured transaction data extracted from them is saved.

File/Storage: TaxWise accesses files only when you explicitly select a document to upload. Files are transmitted to our AI engine for extraction and are not retained beyond the duration of that processing request.

You will be shown a clear explanation before any OS permission prompt is triggered. You may deny these permissions — the app remains functional through manual entry.

8. Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

You have the following rights, exercisable through the Privacy Control Centre in your Profile settings or by contacting our DPO:

  • Right of Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Erasure — Delete your account and personal identifiers (subject to statutory retention of tax records)
  • Right to Data Portability — Export your data in machine-readable format (CSV/JSON)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Withdraw marketing consent at any time without affecting service access
  • Right to Object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Complain — Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng

9. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+
  • All data encrypted at rest in Supabase (AES-256)
  • Row-level security (RLS) — your data is only accessible to you
  • Authentication via Supabase Auth with email verification
  • No staff access to individual user transaction data
  • AI processing uses ephemeral sessions — no document storage

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware, in accordance with NDPA 2023 S.40.

10. Children's Data

TaxWise Nigeria is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact our DPO immediately for erasure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes to this Privacy Policy via email and in-app notification at least 14 days before the changes take effect. Continued use after that date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
